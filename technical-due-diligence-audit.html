Technical due diligence & project audit in Kochi

An independent engineering view of a project's condition, progress and remaining requirements. SAT PMC supports technical due diligence, audit and monitoring for informed project decisions.

WHAT WE BRING

  • An independent condition and progress review
  • Real percentage complete and cost-to-complete assessment
  • A clear scope for technical and compliance questions

Understand what is actually on site

A condition survey and technical review help establish a project's current position. The questions may concern the state of the works, observed progress or the information needed for a development decision. SAT PMC brings civil, MEP, construction and commercial perspectives into the review according to the appointment.

Verify progress and remaining cost

Reported progress needs to be considered against the physical work and the project information available. Our capability includes review of real percentage complete and cost-to-complete assessment. The basis of that assessment matters: the scope, quantities, available records and remaining work need to be understood rather than relying on an unsupported headline percentage.

Review coordination, buildability and compliance

Technical advisory work can include project audits, monitoring and proof-checking of the Design Brief Report. The company profile also describes a value engineering approach that considers structure, façade, MEP and finishes in relation to buildability, maintainability and functionality. Compliance questions should be identified explicitly in the brief so that the required specialist input can be scoped.

A defined review for a defined decision

A due diligence appointment should start with the decision the client needs to make. Share the project location, asset class, available drawings and records, current stage and specific concerns. We can then discuss the condition survey, information review and technical reporting required. Our Kalamassery office provides a Kochi base for appointments across the project locations recorded in our company profile.

Common questions

How is due diligence different from ongoing PMC?+

Due diligence is a defined technical review of the project's position and questions raised by the client. An ongoing PMC appointment coordinates the wider programme, construction and commercial work through delivery.

Can you review a partly completed development?+

Our capability includes real percentage complete and cost-to-complete assessment. Share the current position and available records so that a suitable review can be discussed.
