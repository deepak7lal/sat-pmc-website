WHAT WE BRING

  • A structured prequalification and tender process

Define the tender before comparing the price

Tendering starts with an understanding of the work, the relevant project information and the criteria for selecting delivery partners. SAT PMC supports contractor prequalification, empanelment and tender documentation. Setting the basis clearly helps the project team understand what is being compared when bids arrive.

Evaluate bids and support negotiations

Bid comparison considers the submitted proposals against the tender requirements. Pre-bid and post-bid negotiation, rate discussions and cost-benefit analysis help the client and project team work through commercial questions. EPC pre-bid services are also part of our capability. The appropriate review is defined by the procurement route and the information available.

Keep the contract connected to delivery

Contract administration continues after an award. Programme, budget, procurement and site progress need to be coordinated with the contractual position. SAT PMC combines contracts and quantity surveying expertise with construction management, allowing commercial matters to be reviewed in the context of the work being delivered.

Experienced contracts and quantity surveying leadership

Our Head of Contracts & Quantity Survey, Santhosh V.M., brings tendering and estimation experience across India and abroad, including buildings, transportation, metro and water-sector projects. The company profile records previous experience with Gammon India Ltd. and Centaur Advisory Services, Mumbai. Specialist input is brought into an appointment according to its scope.

Common questions

Can tendering be part of a full PMC appointment?+

Yes. Tendering is one stage of our project lifecycle, between preconstruction and execution. It can be coordinated with planning, cost management and construction oversight.

What should we share for a tender review?+

Provide the procurement route, available tender documents, drawings, BOQ, programme and the specific decisions or commercial questions you need support with.
