Establish the quantity and cost baseline

Detailed estimates and bills of quantities (BOQ) help turn a development brief and design information into a commercial reference. The level of detail depends on the available information and the purpose of the estimate. SAT PMC supports quantification and budget validation so that the project team can discuss scope and cost with a clear starting point.

Connect expenditure with the programme

Cash flow needs to be understood alongside the sequence of construction and procurement. The company profile includes interlinking the construction schedule with the bill of quantities using software. Bringing these views together helps the team examine the monetary effect of time and progress, rather than treating the budget and programme as separate reports.

Track material use and commercial variance

Material reconciliation and cost variance reporting provide continuing visibility during execution. They help identify differences between the planned position and the project records available for review. Cost control also involves reviewing cost-viable proposals and considering risks. The reporting format and frequency should support the client's actual decision process.

Value engineering with the wider team

Cost consultancy works best when coordinated with engineering and construction. SAT PMC's value engineering approach considers structure, façade, MEP and finishes, including buildability, maintainability and functionality. The objective is to assess options in the context of the brief and ongoing operation, not simply to select the lowest initial figure.
