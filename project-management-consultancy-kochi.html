What a project management consultant does

A project management consultant connects the client's objectives with the work of designers, contractors and specialist teams. The role includes establishing a programme, coordinating information, reviewing progress and keeping cost, quality and contractual matters visible. SAT PMC acts as a single point of contact so that decisions can be taken with an understanding of their effect on the wider project.

Support across the project lifecycle

At feasibility, we work with the client brief and design concept. Preconstruction brings cost estimation and budget freezing. Tendering covers the tender process, vendor analysis and award coordination. During execution, project control is connected with cost control, quality management and procurement coordination. Closure includes snag clearance, inspection, testing, commissioning and defect liability period coordination.

An appointment built around your brief

The appropriate team and reporting arrangements depend on the development, the client's existing consultants and the stage at which we join. Base buildings and interiors need different coordination. A commercial complex, institutional fit-out and resort development also have different priorities. We define the appointment around the project rather than treating every engagement as the same package.

Common questions

When should we appoint a PMC?+

Early involvement at feasibility or preconstruction helps align the brief, programme and budget. If work has already started, share the current position so the required support can be defined.

What should we share for an initial discussion?+

Send the site location, development type, approximate project value, current stage, target programme and the services you need. Existing drawings and project information can be discussed once the scope is understood.

Our scope can include

  • Detailed project report and scope coordination
  • Planning, scheduling and progress monitoring
  • Construction supervision and coordination
  • Quality and safety monitoring
  • Cost control and procurement coordination
  • Contract administration and value engineering
  • Project closure and handover
