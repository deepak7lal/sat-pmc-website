Construction planning & scheduling in Kochi

WHAT WE BRING

  • A structured baseline programme

Build a baseline around the actual work

A baseline programme and work breakdown structure (WBS) organise a project into activities that can be planned and monitored. The programme needs to reflect the scope, sequence and interfaces between construction, design information, procurement and specialist work. It provides the reference against which progress and changes can be reviewed.

Monitor progress and identify the next decision

Periodic progress monitoring is most useful when it supports action. SAT PMC reviews progress with the contractor and project team, considers variance against the programme and identifies matters requiring coordination. Review meetings bring the schedule into the same conversation as procurement, available information and the work that can actually proceed.

Assess delay and develop a response

Delay estimation, assessment and mitigation are related but distinct tasks. Understanding what has moved, when it moved and the effect on the programme is the starting point. Mitigation can then be discussed with the team. Fast-track construction and acceleration require careful consideration of methodology, resources, quality and safety rather than a change to dates alone.

Extension of time claim support

Where an extension of time claim is required, the programme and project records form an important part of the assessment. SAT PMC provides delay analysis and claim support. The relevant contract, baseline, updates, correspondence and records need to be understood before an appropriate scope of analysis can be agreed. Planning work can also be coordinated with our contracts and dispute support services.

Common questions

What is the difference between planning and monitoring?+

Planning sets out the intended sequence and programme. Monitoring compares actual progress with that reference and helps the team identify variance, constraints and decisions.

Can scheduling be coordinated with cost control?+

Yes. The company profile includes interlinking the construction schedule and bill of quantities, as well as reviewing schedule and cost variance. The reporting approach is defined for the project.
